About

Most of my work has landed in the same place: taking a model or a sensor and making it behave sensibly in a room full of real objects and real people. With Decormate AR that meant getting furniture to stay where a user put it, on surfaces the phone had only just discovered. With the chest X-ray work it meant building a pipeline that did not fall apart the moment the images came from a different machine.

I am currently pursuing a Master of Science in Computer Science at Northeastern's Khoury College, where I have been taking programming design paradigms, database management systems and algorithms. I am looking for a co-op or internship from January 2027, ideally somewhere I can keep working on systems that have to deal with the physical world.
